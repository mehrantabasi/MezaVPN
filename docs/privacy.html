Privacy Policy

MezaVPN provides Android and Windows VPN connections without advertising SDKs or an account requirement. This page summarizes information used by the official applications; the repository’s complete Privacy Policy is authoritative.

Information stored on the device

Application preferences, connection settings, available server profiles, the selected server, recent quality-test results, update times, and an installation identifier for optional anonymous statistics may be stored in private app storage. Windows may also keep local diagnostics and update files required for troubleshooting and reliable upgrades.

Network activity

MezaVPN makes requests required to retrieve service information, measure connection quality, establish VPN tunnels, and confirm usable internet access. Connectivity checks may contact widely available services operated by providers such as Cloudflare, Google, or Microsoft. Those providers may process standard request information under their own policies.

VPN traffic passes through the selected server. Server operators and destination services may process technical information required to provide their services. Connect only to servers you trust.

Optional anonymous operational statistics

If enabled, MezaVPN may submit a random installation identifier, application version/build, and operating-system platform to measure installations and active versions. On Windows, it may also submit bounded aggregate counters for app launches, connection attempts and outcomes, automatic-mode attempts, unexpected disconnects, and total connected time. This setting can be disabled.

These statistics do not intentionally include browsing activity, traffic content, DNS activity, VPN destinations, selected servers or countries, configuration contents, contacts, files, processes, precise location, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, or hardware identifiers.

Notifications

The Android edition can use Firebase Cloud Messaging for optional announcements. Notification delivery is handled by Google under its applicable terms and privacy practices.

Operational logs

The application and its infrastructure may produce limited technical logs needed for reliability, abuse prevention, or troubleshooting. Hosting and connectivity providers may retain standard access logs under their own policies.

Changes and contact

Material policy changes will be published in the official repository. For privacy questions, open an issue in the official repository without including sensitive information.
